# Research highlight figures

These are original figure assets from the papers referenced by the research
examples. They are stored locally so the website does not hotlink paper images.
The page links each figure to its paper, credits its authors, and offers the
original asset at full size. Astro generates smaller previews for raster images;
the two result plots remain vector graphics. No labels or results were altered.

## Rights and attribution

Paper figures retain their authors' rights; this directory does not relicense
them under the repository's Apache-2.0 code license. FedNCA is distributed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The other five papers
list the [arXiv perpetual, non-exclusive distribution license](https://info.arxiv.org/help/license/index.html).
That license describes arXiv's distribution rights, not a general reuse license.
The selected figures are reproduced here with attribution to illustrate the
specific research discussed on the page. Consult the linked papers and rights
holders for further reuse.

## Maintaining the page

Catalog entries and visible captions live in `web/src/data/research.ts`. Check
each project's local README before describing its runnable scope: a public
starter can use different data or models from its paper. Keep source versions,
figure numbers, credits, and these asset links together when replacing a figure.
